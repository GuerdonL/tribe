# M1 — observations and open questions

2026-11-10, branch `xl-memory`. One run, one seed. Run artifacts (`runs/`) are not in git; they live on the GPU box.

## Setup

- `python -m tribe.train --preset gpu_xl --resume`: XLLearner, d=256, 6 layers, 8 heads, K=32, 10.9M params.
  2048 envs x T=64, 3 epochs x 32 minibatches (64 envs each), lr 2e-4, no remat.
- 3000 updates = 393M env steps in 2.9 h on one RTX 4090 (about 3.5 s/update, about 1 min compile).
- The original windowed `gpu` preset fit in memory (37 GB with remat) but ran about 120 s/update, because it re-encoded a
  32-frame window for every rollout step and every training sample. It was stopped at update 11. The XL path
  caches per-layer memory and runs each env's segment once. `tests/test_train_xl.py` checks that the training pass
  reproduces rollout log-probs exactly (ratio 1 at lr=0).

## Result: pass

`gaze_follow_rate` was above `gaze_chance` in all of the last 500 updates (mean gap +0.071, per-update sd 0.011).
At the end: 0.23 follow vs 0.14 chance, about 5-6k cue steps per update.

Causal check (`scripts/gaze_eval.py`, final weights, 2048 envs x 512 steps, 41,424 cue steps):

| adult position | P(turn R \| faces R) | P(turn R \| faces L) | P(turn L \| faces L) | P(turn L \| faces R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| left of learner  | 0.224 | 0.128 | 0.249 | 0.170 |
| ahead            | 0.303 | 0.177 | 0.338 | 0.206 |
| right of learner | 0.239 | 0.175 | 0.201 | 0.112 |

Flipping only the adult's observed facing (left <-> right), with memory unchanged, shifts
P(matching turn) - P(other turn) by +0.111 (95% CI +0.106..+0.116) when the adult faces right and
+0.123 (+0.117..+0.128) when it faces left. The learner reads the adult's facing, not just where the adult is.

## Observations

1. **Gaze-following rises fast, then fades.** The rolling gap first exceeds +0.02 by update 10, peaks at +0.23
   around updates 200-300, falls to about +0.04 by updates 750-1000, then recovers slowly to +0.07.
2. **Survival keeps improving after the fade.** Deaths per update fall from about 124 to 7, mostly after the gaze peak.
3. **The learner also turns toward the adult's side.** Facing R vs L moves the turn within every position stratum,
   but there is a separate pull toward whichever side the adult is on.
4. **Word prediction falls a lot.** `heard` CE goes 0.50 -> 0.09 (bar was ln 23 = 3.1). World CE plateaus at about 13.
5. **PPO gets more aggressive late.** clipfrac 0.24 -> 0.35 and approx_kl 0.02 -> 0.05 at a constant lr, with no
   instability.
6. **Entropy settles at 1.42.** That is inside the handoff's healthy range (1.0-1.6).

## Open questions

1. **Why does reliance on the adult fade?** Hypothesis: once the learner can find food, water and shelter itself,
   the adult's cue is worth less. Untested. Checking it needs param snapshots over time; `ckpt.pkl` and
   `params.pkl` are overwritten, so only the final weights exist. Next run should keep a snapshot every N updates
   and run `gaze_eval.py` on each.
2. **Does following gaze still help the trained learner survive?** M0 showed it for hand-written policies. For the
   learned one, ablate at eval: blank or randomize the adult's facing/pointing fields and compare deaths and
   needs met. Not done.
3. **Does M2 need sustained reliance on adults?** If later rungs (testimony, language) depend on the learner keeping
   to attend to adults, the fade matters. Levers: harder or larger worlds where the cue stays valuable, danger only
   the adult can see, or adult cues tied to what kills you.
4. **What does `heard` CE 0.09 mean?** It shows the model predicts what the adult says next. It does not show the
   words are grounded. How much is timing or position versus the referent? Compare against a baseline that sees no
   world, or probe word -> object.
5. **Is the result specific to the XL architecture?** Windowed and XL differ in position encoding (relative bias vs
   absolute-in-window) and minibatching (env groups vs random samples). The windowed run only reached 11 updates,
   so there is no comparison. Probably not worth 100 h, but worth noting.
6. **One seed.** The gap is tight within the run, but seed-to-seed variation is unknown. Two more seeds take about 6 h.
7. **Learning rate late in training.** clipfrac 0.35 is high. A linear lr decay or target-KL early stop might keep
   the late policy steadier. Low priority.

## Housekeeping

  `scripts/gaze_eval.py`. Moving the config to its own module would fix this properly.
- `XLBlock` now indexes `rel_bias` through `jnp.asarray`, so numpy params loaded from a pickle work inside `jit`.
